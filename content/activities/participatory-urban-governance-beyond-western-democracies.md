Junyao He is a Guest Co-Editor of the 2027 *Society Register* Special Issue, *Participatory Urban Governance Beyond Western Democracies: Motivations, Expressions, and Outcomes*.

The Special Issue examines how national and local political contexts shape participatory urban governance, including deliberation, implementation, legitimacy, public value, socio-spatial inequality, digital governance, civic technology, resilience, and climate action. It welcomes comparative and context-sensitive research from Africa, Asia, Central and Eastern Europe, South America, and the SWANA region.

**Guest Editors:** Konrad Miciukiewicz, Carolina Aguerre, Kang Cao, Soumyadip Chattopadhyay, Junyao He, Hiroki Nakajima, Przemysław Pluciński, and Elvin Shava.

**Full manuscript deadline:** 30 October 2026  
**Planned publication date:** 30 March 2027  
**Journal:** *Society Register*  
**Access:** Free for authors and readers
