## Blog post

This post introduces our article on how third parties use social media to influence planning processes. The study examines the Enning Road regeneration case in Guangzhou and shows how civil society organizations and journalists mobilize participants, connect online discussion with offline action, and gain relational influence within a constrained planning environment.
