## Blog post

This post presents our study of how players discuss urban planning in online communities around Cities: Skylines. Reddit data reveal both emotional responses to game design and substantive discussion of transport, services, and urban development. The article shows how simulation game communities can provide evidence about public understanding of planning issues.
