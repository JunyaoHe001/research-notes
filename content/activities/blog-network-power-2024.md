## Blog post

This post introduces our article on measuring social network influence in collaborative planning. The study combines social network analysis with interviews to examine the Bell and Drum Tower planning controversy in Beijing. It shows how experts and journalists gain influence through information flows while formal authority remains concentrated in government institutions.
