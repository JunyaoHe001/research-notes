## Research blog

Cambridge Housing Voice published Junyao He's research blog on 14 July 2026. The article examines how remote and hybrid work change the geography of employment, residence, and mobility. It considers the widening of job home relations, the redistribution of housing demand, and the uneven capacity of workers and places to benefit from spatial flexibility.
