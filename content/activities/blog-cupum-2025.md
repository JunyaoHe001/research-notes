## Blog post

CUPUM 2025 brought together researchers working on computational urban planning, digital technologies, and city systems. I presented an agent based model of power dynamics in digital planning, chaired a session, and helped organize a workshop on digital planning and urban AI. The workshop focused on governance, responsibility, and future research priorities.
