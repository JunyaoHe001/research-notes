# Research Agenda

Junyao He's research examines how digitalisation, changing work arrangements, and emerging technologies restructure spatial relations. Complex networks and complex systems provide the common analytical core. Cities, regions, labour markets, migration systems, digital platforms, and governance processes are treated as relational systems whose structure, dynamics, and inequalities evolve over time.

## Urban and economic geography

This strand studies how remote and hybrid work, technological transitions, and changing firm and labour market relations reshape urban systems, regional opportunity, and uneven development.

## Mobility and migration

This strand focuses on job home networks, residential relocation, interregional migration, and digitally enabled mobility. Key questions concern access to spatial flexibility, the geography of attraction and loss, and the distributional effects of new mobility systems.

## Urban planning and governance

This strand examines digital participation, social media, planning controversies, networked power, and the institutionalisation of AI in urban governance.

## Computational social science

Across these empirical fields, Junyao He combines network analysis, spatial econometrics, causal inference, agent based modelling, spatial microsimulation, large scale text analysis, and interpretable machine learning.
